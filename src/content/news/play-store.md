Spark: Escape to Luma is available on Google Play for Android.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.escapetoluma.spark)

Drag to aim, time the opening, and release to launch. Twenty chapters and 150 levels stand between the laboratory and Luma. After that, the journey continues in Endless Voyage.

There is no account. Progress stays on the device.

If you have an iPhone or iPad, the game is on the App Store. [Spark is live on the App Store.](/news/app-store/)
