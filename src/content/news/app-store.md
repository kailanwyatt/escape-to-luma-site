Spark: Escape to Luma is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spark-escape-to-luma/id6816341260)

Drag to aim, time the opening, and release to launch. Twenty chapters and 150 levels stand between the laboratory and Luma. After that, the journey continues in Endless Voyage.

There is no account. Progress stays on the device.

Google Play is not part of this release.
